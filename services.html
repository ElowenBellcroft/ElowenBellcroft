Services

Tailored marketing solutions for every stage of your book's journey.

Every service I offer is fully customized to your specific book, genre, target audience, timeline, and budget. If you are unsure which service is right for your situation, simply send me an email describing your project and I will provide an honest, detailed recommendation with no obligation whatsoever.

01

Full Book Launch Strategy and Execution

A book launch is not a single event that happens on release day. It is a carefully orchestrated, multi-phase campaign that begins months before your book goes live and continues for weeks or even months afterward.

Phase 1: Pre-Launch (3 to 6 Months Before Release)

In-depth competitive analysis of your genre, sub-genre, and top-performing comparable titles to identify market gaps and positioning opportunities. Development of your ideal reader profile including demographics, reading habits, preferred platforms, and purchasing triggers. Comprehensive Amazon keyword and category research to optimize your listing for maximum discoverability in both search results and recommendation algorithms. Creation of your book's marketing positioning statement and core messaging framework that will be used consistently across all channels. Development of a detailed, milestone-driven launch timeline with specific deadlines and deliverables mapped out week by week. Marketing-perspective review of your cover design, title, and blurb with specific optimization recommendations to maximize conversion rates. Setup of pre-order links across all major retail platforms including Amazon, Barnes and Noble, Apple Books, Kobo, and Google Play. Design and implementation of a pre-order incentive campaign to drive early sales and build momentum heading into release week.

Phase 2: ARC and Early Buzz (2 to 3 Months Before Release)

Strategic identification and vetting of genre-specific reviewers, Bookstagrammers, BookTokers, and book bloggers who have demonstrated engagement with books similar to yours. Personalized outreach to 100 to 500+ potential reviewers depending on your genre and goals. Full management of ARC distribution through your preferred platforms. Coordination of early review campaigns to ensure a solid foundation of reviews is live by launch day. Setup of newsletter swaps and cross-promotion opportunities with complementary authors in your genre.

Phase 3: Launch Week

Full coordination of all launch-week activities across every platform and channel. Management of promotional pricing strategies if applicable. Deployment and real-time monitoring of launch-week advertising campaigns on Amazon and Meta with daily bid adjustments. Coordination of social media blitzes including scheduled posts, stories, reels, and community engagement activities. Real-time monitoring of sales data, Amazon rankings, and review accumulation with rapid strategic adjustments.

Phase 4: Post-Launch (1 to 3 Months After Release)

Implementation of a sustained visibility strategy to prevent the post-launch sales cliff. Ongoing advertising campaign management and optimization. Continued reviewer outreach for steady new reviews. Identification of promotional opportunities such as BookBub Featured Deals, newsletter features, and strategic price promotions. Delivery of a comprehensive post-launch performance report with actionable recommendations.

Timeline: Typically begins 3 to 6 months before your release date and extends 1 to 3 months after launch.

Best For: Authors with an upcoming release who want a complete, end-to-end marketing solution.

02

ARC and Review Campaign Management

Reviews are the single most important social proof element in book marketing. If your book launches without a solid foundation of authentic reader reviews, you are fighting an uphill battle from the very first day.

Detailed genre and sub-genre analysis to identify the most active and relevant reviewer communities across all major platforms. Creation of a targeted, vetted reviewer list of 100 to 500+ potential reviewers tailored to your book's specific niche. Crafted outreach email templates that are personalized, professional, and compliant with platform guidelines. Full management of ARC distribution through your preferred platforms including NetGalley, Booksirens, BookFunnel, StoryOrigin, and direct email. Ongoing tracking of reviewer engagement including open rates, download rates, and review conversion rates with weekly progress updates sent to your inbox. Strategic, respectful follow-up sequences designed to maximize review rates. Compilation of all reviews received with direct links, star ratings, and publication dates organized in a comprehensive report. Recommendations for leveraging your reviews in ongoing marketing efforts.

Timeline: Typically begins 2 to 4 months before your release date.

Best For: Authors who understand the critical importance of early reviews but lack the time or contacts for large-scale outreach.

03

Amazon and Meta Advertising Management

Effective book advertising is not about throwing money at a platform and hoping for the best. It is about meticulous keyword research, precise audience targeting, compelling ad creative, strategic bid management, continuous A/B testing, and ongoing optimization based on real performance data.

Amazon Ads Management

Comprehensive keyword research using industry tools and Amazon's own search term data. Setup of Sponsored Products campaigns with both automatic and manual targeting. Setup of Sponsored Brands campaigns if eligible. Setup of Lockscreen Ads for Kindle devices. Ongoing bid optimization to maintain healthy ACOS. Regular A/B testing of targeting strategies, ad copy, and placement options. Weekly performance monitoring and strategic adjustments. Detailed monthly reports showing impressions, clicks, spend, sales, and ACOS broken down by campaign, ad group, and keyword.

Meta Ads Management (Facebook and Instagram)

Audience research and custom audience creation based on reader demographics, interests, behaviors, and lookalike profiles. Setup of conversion, traffic, and engagement campaigns. Creation of compelling ad creative including static image ads, carousel ads, and video ads. Systematic A/B testing of headlines, primary text, images, calls to action, and audience segments. Implementation of retargeting campaigns. Ongoing budget optimization. Detailed monthly performance reports with clear, jargon-free explanations.

Minimum Commitment: 3 months recommended for meaningful data collection and optimization.

Best For: Authors who want to scale book sales through paid advertising without managing campaigns themselves.

04

Social Media and Content Strategy

Social media can be an extraordinarily powerful tool when used strategically, but it can also be a massive time sink that produces virtually no measurable impact on book sales if approached without a clear plan.

Comprehensive audit of your current social media presence including follower demographics, engagement rates, content performance, and alignment with your target readership. Platform recommendation report identifying which 1 to 2 platforms are most likely to produce meaningful results. Detailed content strategy document outlining content pillars, post formats, tone of voice, and visual style. Custom content calendar covering 1 to 3 months of planned posts including post concepts, draft captions, curated hashtag sets, and recommended posting times. Reel and short-form video scripts for BookTok and Instagram Reels including hook formulas, trending audio recommendations, and editing guidance. Engagement strategy covering authentic interaction with readers, fellow authors, and book influencers. Platform-specific growth tactics including collaboration opportunities and community-building strategies.

Timeline: Strategy development 2 to 4 weeks. Ongoing content calendars available on monthly retainer.

Best For: Authors who feel overwhelmed, inconsistent, or frustrated by lack of social media results.

05

Newsletter and Reader Magnet Growth

Your email list is the most valuable marketing asset you will ever own as an author. Unlike social media followers subject to unpredictable algorithms, your email subscribers are people who have explicitly given you permission to contact them directly.

Reader magnet concept development based on deep analysis of your existing books, genre conventions, and target reader preferences. Full copywriting of your reader magnet content if needed, or detailed editorial guidance. Design and formatting guidance to ensure a professional reader experience. Landing page copy and setup guidance for your sign-up funnel, optimized for maximum conversion. Welcome email sequence of 3 to 5 emails that introduces subscribers to you and your work, delivers the reader magnet, and nurtures the relationship toward a purchase. Ongoing newsletter content strategy including recommended sending frequency, content mix, subject line optimization, and engagement tactics. List segmentation strategy for targeted, relevant emails. Integration guidance connecting newsletter growth with your other marketing activities.

Timeline: Initial setup 3 to 6 weeks. Ongoing strategy available on monthly retainer.

Best For: Authors at any career stage who want to build or grow their mailing list.

Author Brand and Positioning Consulting

Your author brand is the total impression that readers, reviewers, booksellers, and industry professionals form when they encounter your name and explore your work.

Comprehensive brand audit of your current online presence. Competitive analysis of 5 to 10 successful authors in your genre. Development of a complete messaging framework including tagline, elevator pitch, short and long-form bios, and brand voice guidelines. Visual brand direction including mood boards, color palette recommendations, typography suggestions, and photography style guidance. Book description and blurb optimization for current and upcoming titles. Amazon Author Central and Goodreads profile optimization recommendations. Comprehensive brand guidelines document for future reference.

Timeline: Typically 4 to 6 weeks.

Best For: Debut authors building from scratch or established authors ready for strategic repositioning.

07

Professional Book Mockup Creation

A flat, two-dimensional cover image simply does not carry the same visual weight or perceived value as a polished, professional three-dimensional book mockup that makes your book look like a tangible, premium product.

Creation of high-resolution 3D book mockups in multiple formats and orientations, including front-facing standalone mockups, angled perspective mockups, flat-lay mockups with genre-appropriate props and backgrounds, e-reader mockups showing your book displayed on a Kindle or tablet screen, and stacked mockups for series or backlist showcases. Genre-appropriate styling and background selection. Multiple file formats delivered to your inbox including PNG with transparent backgrounds, JPG for social media and web use, and high-resolution versions for print materials. Mockups optimized for specific platform dimensions including Instagram posts and stories, Facebook ads, Amazon A+ Content banners, BookBub ad specifications, and newsletter header images. Revision rounds to ensure perfect match with your vision. A usage guide explaining deployment for maximum impact.

Deliverables: 8 to 15 professionally rendered mockup images in multiple formats.

Timeline: 5 to 10 business days from receipt of final cover files.

Best For: Authors who want to elevate the visual quality of all marketing materials.

08

Book Trailer Production and Strategy

Book trailers are short, cinematic video promotions designed to capture the mood, tone, and emotional essence of your book in 30 to 90 seconds of compelling visual storytelling.

Strategic consultation via email to determine ideal trailer style, tone, length, and messaging. Development of a detailed creative brief and storyboard. Scriptwriting for text overlays and voiceover narration if applicable, crafted to hook the viewer within the first three seconds. Selection and licensing of royalty-free music and sound effects. Professional video editing and production using high-quality stock footage, typography, and visual effects. Creation of multiple trailer versions optimized for different platforms: full-length (60 to 90 seconds) for website and YouTube, short-form (15 to 30 seconds) for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook ads, and square-format for Instagram feed posts and Twitter/X. Subtitle and caption integration for all versions to ensure accessibility. Strategic deployment recommendations detailing exactly where, when, and how to use each version.

Timeline: 2 to 4 weeks from creative brief approval to final delivery.

Best For: Authors who want a high-impact visual marketing asset for advertising and promotional newsletters.

09

Goodreads Listopia Campaign

Goodreads remains one of the most influential platforms in the book discovery ecosystem, with over 150 million members worldwide. Listopia lists are actively browsed by millions of readers specifically looking for their next read.

Comprehensive Listopia research to identify the most relevant, high-traffic lists in your genre and sub-genre, including analysis of member count, voting activity, moderation policies, and submission requirements. Competitive analysis of which lists your comparable authors appear on. Strategic list selection and prioritization. Management of the submission process for each targeted list. Vote generation strategy with ethical guidance on encouraging reader support without violating Goodreads' terms of service. Ongoing monitoring of your book's position on targeted lists with regular progress updates. Recommendations for additional Goodreads optimization strategies including author profile enhancement, Goodreads Giveaway setup, and engagement with relevant Goodreads Groups.

Timeline: Research and submission 2 to 4 weeks, ongoing monitoring 2 to 3 months.

Best For: Authors who want to increase visibility on Goodreads through strategic Listopia placement.

10

Book Club Litopia Outreach and Placement

Book club members are voracious readers who purchase books in bulk, discuss them passionately, and frequently recommend their favorites. A single book club selection can generate dozens or even hundreds of guaranteed sales.

Research and identification of active book club communities, networks, and platforms relevant to your genre and target readership. Development of a professional book club kit including a compelling book summary, author biography, discussion questions, and a personal note from you to the book club members. Outreach campaign management to pitch your book to targeted book clubs, coordinators, and influencers across multiple platforms including Book Club Litopia, Book Club Hub, Reading Group Choices, and genre-specific networks. Coordination of any promotional offers or incentives for book clubs that select your book, such as discounted bulk pricing, exclusive bonus content, or author Q and A sessions conducted via email. Tracking and reporting of all book club placements including estimated member counts and attributable sales data. Ongoing relationship management with book club organizers for repeat selections with future releases.

Timeline: Initial outreach 3 to 6 weeks, ongoing tracking 3 to 6 months.

Best For: Authors who want to tap into the powerful, sustainable sales channel of book club selections.
