Why Email Is the Best Way to Reach Me

I conduct all client communication exclusively through Gmail. This is not a limitation. It is a feature. Email ensures that every detail of our conversation is permanently documented, clearly organized, and easily accessible for future reference. When you email me, you will receive a detailed, personalized reply from me directly. Not a generic auto-response, not a redirect to a booking calendar, and not a reply from an assistant.

What to Include in Your Email

Your name (first name or initials are fine). Your genre and sub-genre. Your publishing path (traditional, indie, hybrid, or undecided). Your release date if applicable. Your current marketing efforts. Your current platform size. Your primary marketing goals. Your approximate marketing budget. Any specific questions or concerns.

Do not worry if you cannot answer all of these right now. Share whatever you can and I will work with what you provide.
