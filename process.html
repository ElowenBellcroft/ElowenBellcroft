How I Work

Transparent, organized, and entirely email-based.

1

Your Initial Email

Send me an email at elowenbellcroft@gmail.com with as much detail as you can provide about your book, your genre, your release timeline, your current marketing efforts, your existing platform, and your goals. The more information you include, the more specific and useful my response will be. If you are not sure what to include, do not worry. I will ask you follow-up questions in my reply.

2

My Detailed Response (Within 48 Hours)

I will reply with a thoughtful, comprehensive email that includes my initial assessment of your situation, my honest recommendations for which services would be most impactful for your specific book, any questions I need answered before preparing a formal proposal, and a realistic overview of what you can expect in terms of timeline and investment.

3

The Formal Proposal (Within 3 to 5 Business Days)

Once I have all the information I need, I will send you a detailed proposal document via email that outlines the exact scope of work, the project timeline, the specific deliverables you will receive, and the total investment. You can take as much time as you need to review it, ask any questions you have via email, and decide whether you would like to move forward. There is zero pressure and zero obligation.

4

Onboarding

Once you confirm via email that you would like to proceed, I will send you a comprehensive onboarding package that includes a detailed project questionnaire, a list of any materials or account access I need from you, and a complete project timeline with specific milestones. From that point forward, all project updates, reports, strategy documents, and communications will flow through our email thread in a clear, consistent, and meticulously organized manner.

5

Ongoing Communication and Reporting

Throughout the duration of our project, you can expect regular email updates from me, typically weekly or biweekly depending on the specific service, covering campaign performance data, upcoming planned activities, any decisions that require your input, and strategic recommendations based on the latest results. I am always just one email away if you have questions, concerns, or new ideas between scheduled updates. My standard response time for client emails is within 24 hours on business days.
