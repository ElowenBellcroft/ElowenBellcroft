Selected campaign results from real client projects.

Author identities are represented by initials only to respect client confidentiality. Book titles have been omitted for the same reason. Contemporary Romance M.R.

Full Book Launch Strategy and Execution

12-week engagement

The Situation

M.R. came to me approximately eight weeks before her debut release date. She had invested in professional editing and cover design but had done no marketing preparation whatsoever. She had no pre-orders set up, no reviewer contacts, no mailing list, no social media following beyond a small personal Instagram account, and no experience with advertising.

What I Did

I developed and executed a rapid-response launch plan. I set up pre-order links across all major retailers, rewrote her Amazon listing with targeted keywords and a conversion-optimized book description, launched an ARC campaign targeting 250+ romance-specific reviewers, built a reader magnet funnel, launched Amazon Ads, coordinated a social media push with Bookstagrammers and BookTokers, activated newsletter swaps with established romance authors, and created professional 3D book mockups for all advertising and social media materials.

The Results

92Reviews in 14 days
Top 100Amazon category rank
1,400New subscribers in 30 days
4,200+BookBub deal downloads
Psychological Thriller J.T.

Amazon and Meta Advertising Management

6-month retainer

28%ACOS (from 65%)
300%Backlist sales increase
4.2xMeta Ads ROAS

Cozy Mystery L.W.

8 weeks strategy plus 3 months content calendars
