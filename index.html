The Uncomfortable Truth About Publishing Right Now

Over 4 million new books are published every single year across all formats and platforms. Amazon alone receives thousands of new uploads every single day. The marketplace has never been more crowded, and the harsh reality is that writing an exceptional book is no longer enough to guarantee that anyone will discover it, purchase it, or leave a review.

Most authors spend anywhere from six months to several years drafting, revising, editing, and polishing their manuscript. They invest significant money in professional cover design, developmental editing, copyediting, and formatting. And then launch day arrives. They hit "publish," share a post or two on social media, notify their friends and family, and wait.

And then nothing happens.

The Amazon ranking drops within 48 hours. The reviews never materialize. The sales flatline at a handful of copies per week. The algorithm buries the listing beneath thousands of newer releases. And the author is left feeling exhausted, defeated, and convinced that they are simply "bad at marketing."

But here is the truth. You are not bad at marketing. Marketing is an entirely separate professional discipline from writing. It requires expertise in audience research, keyword optimization, advertising platforms, email funnel architecture, social media algorithms, reviewer outreach ecosystems, pricing psychology, category placement strategy, and launch timing.

That is exactly why I exist. You focus on the writing. I focus on making sure the right readers find your work.
